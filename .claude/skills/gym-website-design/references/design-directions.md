# Design directions in detail

Each direction is a starting point. Make it specific to the client: their space, their members, their city.
Avoid the generic defaults (near-black + neon accent, cream + serif + terracotta, all-caps labels above every
heading, identical rounded cards everywhere, arrows on every button).

## 1. Premium Fitness

**Who:** luxury clubs, boutique strength/Pilates, high-ticket personal training, wellness + recovery.
**Feeling:** calm, confident, exclusive, unhurried.

- **Layout:** asymmetric editorial grids, large images with generous margins, fewer sections with more
  air. Text blocks narrow (max ~60ch). Left-aligned.
- **Typography:** one refined sans (e.g. "Instrument Sans", "Manrope", "Hanken Grotesk") or a restrained
  serif display paired with a sans. Light to medium weights for display, large sizes, slightly open tracking.
- **Colour:** 3–4 neutrals (stone, ink, warm grey or cool slate) + one muted accent (bronze, deep green,
  oxblood). Accent used for CTAs only.
- **Photography:** architecture, light, texture of equipment, single subjects; consistent colour grade.
- **Components:** amenity cards, "from" pricing or tiers by consultation, enquiry form with preferred visit
  time, coach profiles with credentials.
- **CTA wording:** "Book a visit", "Enquire about membership", "Arrange a consultation".
- **Motion:** slow fades (400–700 ms), subtle image scale on load; nothing bouncy.
- **Risks:** too little information. Still show location, hours and a clear next step.

## 2. High-Energy Fitness

**Who:** HIIT, boxing, bootcamp, strength & conditioning, functional/CrossFit-style, class studios.
**Feeling:** intense, motivating, communal, fast.

- **Layout:** full-bleed hero, strong diagonals or big type crops, dense schedule, punchy section rhythm.
- **Typography:** heavy, expanded or condensed display ("Archivo" at wide widths, "Anton", "Bebas Neue",
  "Barlow Condensed") with a clean body face. Tight line-height (0.9–1.05) on headlines.
- **Colour:** dark base + one saturated accent; optionally use extra colours **only to encode meaning**
  (e.g. intensity levels coloured like competition weight plates).
- **Photography:** action, sweat, motion blur, close crops, high contrast; groups training together.
- **Components:** class schedule with filters, intro offer, class formats, coach cards, testimonials,
  "what to expect" for first-timers.
- **CTA wording:** "Book your first class", "Claim a free session", "See the schedule".
- **Motion:** one orchestrated hero moment; quick, responsive UI feedback (150–250 ms).
- **Risks:** intimidating beginners. Add reassurance: levels, scaling, beginner-friendly classes.

## 3. Commercial Gym

**Who:** budget and mid-market gyms, 24/7 access clubs, community and family gyms, multi-location chains.
**Feeling:** welcoming, practical, clear, good value.

- **Layout:** predictable sections, clear navigation, prominent price table, location/hours blocks, FAQ.
- **Typography:** highly legible humanist or grotesque sans ("Public Sans", "Figtree", "Nunito Sans"),
  medium to bold headings, body ≥ 17px.
- **Colour:** bright brand colour with plenty of white; button colour reserved for actions; AA contrast.
- **Photography:** real members of different ages, sizes and abilities; bright, friendly; the actual floor.
- **Components:** tier comparison table, amenities icon grid, opening hours, free day pass, FAQ, location
  pages, accessibility information.
- **CTA wording:** "Join now", "Get a free day pass", "Find your gym".
- **Motion:** almost none; prioritise speed.
- **Risks:** looking generic. Use the client's own photos and local identity to differentiate.

## Mixing directions

Choose one as base; borrow one trait. Examples:
- Commercial + one High-Energy trait → intensity-coded class schedule.
- Premium + one High-Energy trait → bold condensed hero headline over an editorial layout.
- High-Energy + one Commercial trait → transparent pricing table with a free day pass.
