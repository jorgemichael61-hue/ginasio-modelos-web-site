# Client intake: information required before a gym site can go live

Send this list to the client. Nothing here should be invented.

## Business
- Legal and trading name, logo files (SVG preferred), brand colours and fonts if any
- Positioning: who the gym is for, what makes it different, price level
- Year opened, awards, affiliations (only verifiable ones)

## Location and contact
- Full address, map pin/coordinates, parking, public transport, accessibility of the building
- Phone, WhatsApp, email, contact person for leads
- Opening hours (staffed and unstaffed/24-7 if different), holiday hours

## Offer
- Services/programmes with descriptions, duration, level, who they suit
- Weekly class schedule (day, time, duration, coach, room, level), booking rules
- Membership plans: prices, billing period, joining fee, minimum term, cancellation terms, discounts
- Trial offer / day pass / intro pack: price and conditions
- Personal training packages and prices

## People and proof
- Trainers: name, role, photo, specialties, certifications, short bio, consent to publish
- Testimonials with written permission (name/initials, context); Google review link

## Media
- Professional photos/video of the real space and members (with model releases), or approval for stock photos

## Systems
- Booking platform and links (Mindbody, Glofox, PushPress, TeamUp…)
- Online sign-up / payment provider
- Form destination email or form service account
- Domain name and DNS access, hosting choice
- Analytics (GA4, Plausible), Google Business Profile access, social media links

## Legal
- Privacy policy, cookie policy, terms and cancellation policy (client or their lawyer must approve)
- Company registration/VAT details if required in the footer
